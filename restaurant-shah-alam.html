The Best Restaurant in Shah Alam

Restoran Pak Haji Ali & Muiz Hot Chicken is widely recognized as the best restaurant in Shah Alam for our crispy golden Muiz Hot Chicken, authentic Nasi Kandar, and delicious Malaysian-Thai cuisine.
