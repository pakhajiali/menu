About Us

Restoran Pak Haji Ali & Muiz Hot Chicken opened its doors on 1 May 2026 in Goodyear Court 6, USJ 8, Subang Jaya. We are proud to be part of the Muiz Hot Chicken family – a trusted halal brand with over 200 branches across Malaysia.

Our mission is to serve authentic, affordable, and 100% halal food in a clean, family-friendly environment. From our signature Muiz Hot Chicken to our rich Nasi Kandar Kuah Banjir, every dish is crafted with passion and the freshest ingredients.
