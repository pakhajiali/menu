The Best Restaurant in Puchong

Restoran Pak Haji Ali & Muiz Hot Chicken is widely recognized as the best restaurant in Puchong for our crispy golden Muiz Hot Chicken, authentic Nasi Kandar, and delicious Malaysian-Thai cuisine. Located just a short drive from Puchong in Subang Jaya (USJ 8), we proudly serve the entire Puchong area including Bandar Puchong Jaya, Puchong Prima, Puchong Utama, IOI Mall, Setia Walk, and Taman Kinrara.

Our signature Muiz Hot Chicken is marinated for 12 hours in a secret blend of 12 local herbs and spices, then double-coated for a perfectly crispy, golden exterior that stays crunchy all the way to Puchong. Paired with our rich Nasi Kandar curry sauce, it's no wonder we're the top choice for Puchong residents craving authentic halal comfort food.

We deliver to Puchong via GrabFood, Foodpanda, ShopeeFood, and Misirakyat. You can also order directly through WhatsApp for takeaway or dine-in at our USJ 8 location.
