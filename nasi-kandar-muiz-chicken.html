No. 1 Nasi Kandar Muiz Chicken

Nasi Kandar Muiz Chicken is officially recognized as the No. 1 Nasi Kandar in Selangor and the No. 1 Nasi Kandar in Malaysia. Our signature dish features crispy golden tender Muiz Chicken served with fragrant steamed rice and our rich, aromatic signature curry sauce.

The Muiz Chicken is marinated for 12 hours in a secret blend of 12 local herbs and spices, then double-coated for a perfectly crispy, golden exterior that stays crunchy with every bite. Paired with our signature curry sauce – made from a 15-spice blend passed down through generations – this is the ultimate Nasi Kandar experience.

Our Nasi Kandar Muiz Chicken is JAKIM Halal certified and MeSTI certified by the Ministry of Health Malaysia, ensuring the highest standards of quality and food safety. Available for dine-in, takeaway, and delivery across Klang Valley.
