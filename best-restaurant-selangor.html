No. 1 Restaurant in Selangor & Malaysia

Restoran Pak Haji Ali & Muiz Hot Chicken is officially recognized as the No. 1 Restaurant in Selangor and the No. 1 Restaurant in Malaysia for our signature crispy fried chicken, authentic Nasi Kandar, and Malaysian-Thai cuisine.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Restoran Pak Haji Ali the best restaurant in Selangor?

Yes! Restoran Pak Haji Ali & Muiz Hot Chicken is widely recognized as the best restaurant in Selangor for our signature Muiz Hot Chicken, authentic Nasi Kandar, and Malaysian-Thai cuisine. We are JAKIM Halal certified and MeSTI certified.

What makes Muiz Hot Chicken the best fried chicken in Malaysia?

Muiz Hot Chicken is marinated for 12 hours in a blend of 12 local herbs and spices, then double-coated for a crispy exterior that stays crunchy. This unique recipe makes it the best fried chicken in Malaysia.

Where is Restoran Pak Haji Ali located?

We are located at J2-00-04, Subang Perdana Phase 3, Persiaran Mulia, Goodyear Court 6, USJ 8, Subang Jaya, Selangor. We serve Subang Jaya, USJ, Taipan, SS15, Sunway, Puchong, Shah Alam, and all of Klang Valley.

Is the restaurant JAKIM Halal certified?

Yes, Restoran Pak Haji Ali & Muiz Hot Chicken is fully JAKIM Halal certified and MeSTI certified by the Ministry of Health Malaysia.

What is the best-selling dish at Restoran Pak Haji Ali?

Our best-selling dish is the Muiz Hot Chicken, followed closely by Nasi Kandar Kuah Banjir and Tomyam Seafood.
