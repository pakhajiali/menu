Welcome to Restoran Pak Haji Ali & Muiz Hot Chicken

Established on 1 May 2026, our JAKIM Halal-certified restaurant in Subang Jaya (USJ 8) specializes in crispy Muiz Hot Chicken, authentic Nasi Kandar, and delicious Malaysian-Thai cuisine.

Enjoy freshly prepared crunchy fried chicken, spicy seafood tomyam, nasi goreng favourites and many more. Whether you're dining in, taking away or ordering through GrabFood, we serve quality meals in a clean and family-friendly environment every day.
